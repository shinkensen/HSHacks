# MomentumAI

 MomentumAI is a mental wellbeing web application centered on personal growth, both individually and through meaningful connections with others. By combining AI guidance, physical activity, and task completion, MomentumAI creates a holistic system that helps users build healthier habits, stay motivated, and feel more in control of their lives. Whether someone is looking to improve their mental health or maintain it, MomentumAI provides a structured yet engaging experience.

## Wellbeing: Ability To Exercise with Friends And Family
 MomentumAI uses AI-powered motion recognition to track exercises such as push-ups and curl-ups, whether performed individually or with others on a Local Area Network (LAN). Users can easily create or join lobbies using simple codes, making it seamless to work out together.
The platform enhances this experience with real-time voice chat and a live leaderboard, allowing users to compete, encourage one another, and stay motivated. This combination transforms exercise into a social and engaging activity rather than a solitary task.

 ## Wellbeing: Personal Task Completion with AI 
MomentumAI’s AI system acts as a mentor, helping users break down complex or overwhelming tasks into manageable steps. By analyzing inputs—whether text, images, or video—the AI provides structured guidance tailored to the user’s needs. The more context users provide, the more precise and helpful the recommendations become. This allows users to approach challenges with clarity and confidence, reducing stress and increasing productivity.

 ## How Does MomentumAI Positively Impact Wellbeing? 
 MomentumAI improves wellbeing by combining social interaction, physical activity, and smart task guidance. Voice chat and friend leaderboards make self-improvement more engaging and collaborative. Exercise supports mental health by reducing stress, anxiety, and depression while boosting mood and focus. Completing tasks builds confidence, lowers stress, and reinforces motivation through a sense of accomplishment.
 
 ## Technical Stack
 Frontend: Next.js 
 Storage/Database: Convex and Upstash Redis
 Authentication: Clerk 
 Motion Recognition: Mediapipe 
 AI Companion: GPT 5.4 Nano  
 Voice Chat SDK: Agora 

 ## Features that we are proud of 
 Seamless LAN-based lobby creation and joining
 Real-time repetition tracking across users
 Reliable, low-latency voice communication
 Integrated AI-driven task assistance

 ## Some Problems we ran into
 One of the biggest challenges was implementing accurate motion detection. Integrating MediaPipe with our existing frontend required careful coordination to ensure real-time responsiveness and reliability. Achieving smooth, seamless interaction between the motion recognition system and the user interface was critical—and ultimately one of the most rewarding aspects of development.
