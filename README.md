# momentum.ai

momentum.ai is a mental wellbeing web application centered on personal growth, both individually and through meaningful connections with others. By combining AI guidance, physical activity, and task completion, momentum.ai creates a holistic system that helps users build healthier habits, stay motivated, and feel more in control of their lives. Whether someone is looking to improve their mental health or maintain it, momentum.ai provides a structured yet engaging experience.

## Wellbeing: Ability To Exercise with Friends And Family

momentum.ai uses AI-powered motion recognition to track exercises such as push-ups and curl-ups, whether performed individually or with others on a Local Area Network (LAN). Users can easily create or join lobbies using simple codes, making it seamless to work out together.
The platform enhances this experience with real-time voice chat and a live leaderboard, allowing users to compete, encourage one another, and stay motivated. This combination transforms exercise into a social and engaging activity rather than a solitary task.

## Wellbeing: Personal Task Completion with AI

momentum.ai’s AI system acts as a mentor, helping users break down complex or overwhelming tasks into manageable steps. By analyzing inputs—whether text, images, or video—the AI provides structured guidance tailored to the user’s needs. The more context users provide, the more precise and helpful the recommendations become. This allows users to approach challenges with clarity and confidence, reducing stress and increasing productivity.

## How Does momentum.ai Positively Impact Wellbeing?

momentum.ai improves wellbeing by combining social interaction, physical activity, and smart task guidance. Voice chat and friend leaderboards make self-improvement more engaging and collaborative. Exercise supports mental health by reducing stress, anxiety, and depression while boosting mood and focus. Completing tasks builds confidence, lowers stress, and reinforces motivation through a sense of accomplishment.

## Technical Stack

Frontend: Next.js  
Storage/Database: Convex and Upstash Redis  
Authentication: Clerk  
Motion Recognition: Mediapipe  
AI Companion: GPT 5.4 Nano  
Voice Chat SDK: Agora

## Features that we are proud of

Seamless LAN-based lobby creation and joining  
Real-time repetition tracking across users  
Reliable, low-latency voice communication  
Integrated AI-driven task assistance
